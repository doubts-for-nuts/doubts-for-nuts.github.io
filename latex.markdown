I became interested in document composition using $$\TeX$$ when I wrote my B.Sc. thesis, and I haven’t stopped since. I designed and taught courses on the $$\LaTeX$$ language at the universities of Ferrara and Pavia, Italy, at bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral levels for six years.

Over time, I have completed various projects aimed at academic and corporate audiences, while others I undertook purely for personal satisfaction. I have selected a few of these for this portfolio.

Every element of these pages, whether text or graphics, was created by me using only software and packages from the $$\TeX$$ family.
